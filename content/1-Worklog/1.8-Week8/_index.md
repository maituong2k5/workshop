## Week 8 Objectives:

Explore containerization technologies and AWS container services, including Docker, Amazon ECS, and Lightsail Containers. Understand their purposes and deployment models, and evaluate their applicability to the Phone Store project.

## Week 8 Achievements:

- Understood the basic concepts and benefits of application containerization.
- Learned about Docker images, containers, and Dockerfiles.
- Understood the main components and operating principles of Amazon ECS.
- Explored AWS Lightsail Containers and its deployment model.
- Compared different AWS application deployment approaches.
- Evaluated container technologies and identified that the Phone Store project does not use Docker, Kubernetes, Amazon ECS, or Lightsail Containers in its current deployment architecture.
