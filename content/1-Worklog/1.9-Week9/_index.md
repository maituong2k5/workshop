## Week 9 Objectives:

Understand how Amazon CloudWatch monitors AWS resources and how EC2 Auto Scaling automatically adjusts the number of EC2 instances based on application demand.

## Week 9 Achievements:

- Understood the role of Amazon CloudWatch in monitoring AWS resources and applications.
- Learned about CloudWatch Metrics, Logs, and Alarms.
- Understood how to monitor EC2 instance performance.
- Learned the basic concepts and operating principles of EC2 Auto Scaling.
- Understood Launch Templates, Auto Scaling Groups, and scaling policies.
- Evaluated how monitoring and automatic scaling can support the availability and performance of the TechVina application.
